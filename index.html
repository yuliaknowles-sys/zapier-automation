Behind the scenes

What happens when you press send

Zapier connects apps that don't normally talk to each other. Each rule is called a Zap: when something happens in one place, it does something in another. One form submission sets off three Zaps at the same time.

1

Request comes in

A visitor fills in the form: name, email, what they need, and any details.

Zapier Forms
2

Logged

The request is added to a tracker with the date and time, so every request sits in one list.

Zapier Tables
3

I'm alerted

I get an email with the details right away, so the request is visible before I open the inbox.

Email by Zapier
4

Sender hears back

They receive a personal confirmation saying I have their request and when to expect my answer.

Email by Zapier

Before

  • Requests arrive by text, email and phone
  • Details copied into a tracker by hand
  • The sender waits until someone replies
  • Hard to see later who asked for what, and when

After

  • One form for every request
  • Logged automatically with a time stamp
  • The sender gets a confirmation within minutes
  • A full record to review at any time

In a private office

How I would use this for a principal and a household

The website form is only the example. The same three steps (capture, log, respond) work anywhere requests reach the principal's office or the house.

Household

Maintenance and vendor requests

Staff report an issue at the residence, like a pool heater or a gate that won't close, from their phone.

Logged with the property and room · I'm alerted · the vendor gets the details and a request to confirm a visit
Principal's guests

Guest arrivals and house stays

Guests submit arrival times, flight numbers and dietary notes before a visit.

Logged in the guest list · house staff are alerted · the guest receives a welcome note with arrival details
Events

RSVPs for private events

Invitees reply through one form instead of a mix of texts and emails.

Headcount updated · caterer notified of dietary needs · each guest gets a confirmation
Staff

Time-off and schedule requests

Household staff request days off or shift swaps in one place.

Logged in the staff calendar tracker · the estate manager is alerted · the staff member gets a "received" note

How it's built

Tools and design choices

Zapier FormsThe request form, hosted by Zapier and embedded on this page.
Zapier TablesThe tracker where every request is stored with a time stamp.
Email by ZapierThe alert to me and the confirmation to the sender.

Zapier's free plan allows one action per Zap, so instead of one long chain I built three short Zaps that all start from the same form. It runs at no cost, and if one step ever fails, the other two still go through.
